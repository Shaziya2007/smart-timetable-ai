## **Problem Description**

The **Smart Timetable Generator Portal** is a comprehensive web-based application designed to digitize and automate the timetable creation process in educational institutions. The system provides **role-based access** for administrators, faculty members, and users, enabling secure management of courses, faculty, classrooms, subjects, and schedules.

The portal automates timetable generation while considering institutional constraints, reduces manual effort, minimizes scheduling conflicts, generates analytical reports, sends real-time notifications, and integrates securely with institutional systems. This improves **operational efficiency, transparency, collaboration, and overall user experience.**
